Activity 2.5.3. More Inequalities.

(a)

Solve the inequality \(\abs{2x-5} \le 6\) in two ways

  1. Graph \(y=\abs{2x-5}\) on the grid and use the graph to solve the inequality. Write your answer in interval notation.

  2. Solve algebraically. \(\abs{2x-5} \le 6\) is equivalent to \(-6 \le 2x-5 \le 6\)

(b)

Solve the inequality \(\abs{3x-6} \ge 3\) in two ways

  1. Graph \(y=\abs{3x-6}\) on the grid and use the graph to solve the inequality. Write your answer in interval notation.

  2. Solve algebraically. \(\abs{3x-6} \ge 3\) is equivalent to \(3x-6 \ge 3\) or \(3x-6 \le -3\)
