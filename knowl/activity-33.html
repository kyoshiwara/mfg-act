Activity 2.6.2. Another Triangle.

A right triangle is formed in the first quadrant as follows: Two of the sides are formed by the \(x\)- and \(y\)-axes, the third side is on a line passing through the point \((2,1)\text{.}\) (Note that this is the same picture as in Activity 1.)

(a)

Let \((a,0)\) be the \(x\)-intercept of the hypotenuse of the triangle. Express the slope of the hypotenuse in terms of \(a\text{.}\)

(b)

Let \((0,b)\) be the \(y\)-intercept of the third side. Express \(b\) in terms of \(a\text{.}\) (Use your expression for the slope.

(c)

Express the area of the triangle as a function of \(a\text{,}\) \(A=f(a)\text{.}\)

(d)

What is the domain of your function? (What values of \(a\) result in a triangle?)

(e)

Use technology to graph your function on the domain \((2,20.8]\text{.}\) Make a sketch of your graph.

(f)

What is the area of the triangle if \(a=3\text{?}\)

What value(s) of \(a\) result in a triangle of area 7.2 square units?

(g)

What value of \(a\) results in the triangle with the smallest area?

What is the area of that triangle?

(h)

Describe what happens to the area of the triangle as \(a\) increases from 2 to larger values. What is the range of the function \(A=f(a)\text{?}\)
