This is a longish Lesson that reviews some skills from algebra in the context of applications: solving simple equations and inequalities, evaluating expressions, and graphing an equation by plotting points. It's a good idea to work through Lesson 1.1 for the class, so that they have a model to follow for future Lessons. Show students how you want them to record their work. You may wish to budget more than one day for this section as students settle into the class and refresh their skills.
